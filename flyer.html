📖 Overview

EduSL is a comprehensive, cloud-based school management system designed for the Sierra Leonean education sector. It connects administrators, teachers, bursars, parents, and students through a unified, intuitive interface.

✅ Role-Based Access
Admin · Bursar · Teacher · Parent · Student
✅ Real-Time Sync
Firebase Firestore · Offline Support
✅ Mobile Responsive
Works on phone, tablet & desktop
✅ Secure & Private
Authentication · Tenant isolation

⭐ Standout Features

🤖

AI Chat Assistant

Students and parents can ask questions about attendance, fees, exams, and school notices — powered by intelligent automation.

📱

Mobile Responsive

Access the system from any device — phone, tablet, or desktop — with a fully responsive, touch-friendly interface.

🔐

Role-Based Security

Fine-grained access control ensures that admins, teachers, bursars, parents, and students see only what they need.

📊

Real-Time Analytics

Interactive charts and KPI dashboards provide instant insights into attendance, fees, exam performance, and more.

📧

Email Notifications

Automated email alerts for attendance, fees, exams, staff payroll, admissions, and suggestions keep everyone informed.

💬

Voice Notes & Replies

Teachers and parents can attach voice notes to attendance records and reply with voice or text for better communication.

📄

Document Exchange

Admins upload documents for students; parents reply with files — a secure two-way document sharing system.

🌍

School Discovery

Parents and students can browse registered schools, view details, and request admission directly through the platform.

📋

Report Card Generator

Generate professional, printable report cards with exam results, attendance summaries, and fee status for any term.
